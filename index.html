AI Generalist for L&D Professionals

Mastering AI Feedback Iteration

Generative AI rarely delivers a ready-to-use output on the first attempt. The key to high-impact learning and performance conversations lies in strategic iteration.

The 5 Principles of Feedback Iteration

Explore each principle below to see how you can guide AI when drafting capability assessments, coaching guides, learner feedback, or stakeholder evaluations.

The Mindset Shift for L&D

Before integrating AI, crafting developmental feedback or coaching notes relied on your subconscious expertise. Your brain automatically drew upon adult learning principles, emotional intelligence, and organisational culture.

AI lacks your professional intuition and organisational context. The core capability isn't just writing a clever initial prompt—it is consciously surfacing your tacit L&D expertise and explicitly directing the AI to apply it.

Traditional Intuitive Drafting

  • • You start with a blank page and build the narrative from scratch.
  • • You subconsciously weave in behavioural frameworks and organisational nuance.
  • • Primary Focus: Generating the initial copy.

AI-Enabled Explicit Direction

  • • The AI produces a rapid, structured, yet generic first draft.
  • • You critically evaluate the output and inject human empathy, role context, and psychological safety.
  • • Primary Focus: Shaping and curating the AI's reasoning.

Structuring Iteration Prompts

Treat this as a collaborative partnership: You are the expert in human behaviour and learning (empathy, organisational culture, capability building), and the AI is the engine for synthesis (structuring, drafting, pattern recognition). Use these prompts to combine your L&D expertise with the AI's processing power.

L&D Objective How to Prompt the AI... How Your L&D Expertise Guides the Output
Mitigating Bias "Review this draft against the raw observation notes I provided. Highlight any subjective assumptions or evaluative judgements that aren't directly supported by behavioural evidence." AI often fills gaps with plausible-sounding generalisations. Your prompt forces it to separate objective, observable data from inferred bias.
Fostering Psychological Safety "Adopt the persona of a conscientious team member who struggles with imposter syndrome. Which phrase in this feedback is most likely to trigger a defensive reaction, and how can we reframe it?" AI does not experience emotion. By defining a specific learner persona, you leverage your emotional intelligence to audit and calibrate the tone.
Driving Behavioural Action "This draft identifies the capability gap, but remains too abstract. Suggest three concrete, observable micro-habits the individual can practise during their next project meeting." AI defaults to high-level platitudes. Your understanding of workplace transfer directs the AI to translate theory into practical on-the-job application.
Embedding Workplace Context "Adjust this developmental plan to account for the fact that the team is currently navigating a major system migration and competing project deadlines. How can we make this achievable?" AI generates text in an operational vacuum. Injecting the reality of current organisational pressures ensures learning expectations remain realistic and supportive.
Designing Coaching Conversations "Rather than prescribing the solution, generate three open-ended, reflective questions a manager could ask to help the individual self-diagnose this workflow bottleneck." AI is trained to provide answers. Your facilitation expertise recognises that guided discovery builds stronger capability, shifting the AI from 'advisor' to 'coaching partner'.
